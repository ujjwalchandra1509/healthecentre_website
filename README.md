## Description
Project: Campus AI Healthcare Agent 

Problem: Health services at IIT Bhilai are hard to use. Clinic hours clash with student schedules, so queues are long and consultations get delayed. Info is scattered across notices and websites, booking is manual (phone, paper intake), and lab updates and follow-ups get missed.

Solution: An AI healthcare agent for students and staff. A user asks a question or checks in, and the agent routes it:
Chatbot and symptom checker for quick guidance, 24/7
Automated appointment scheduling
Clinic staff triage and clinician consultation when needed
Pre-filled patient data at the Health Centre to cut repeat paperwork

Benefits:
Faster access to care and shorter queues
Confidential, non-judgmental chat
Reminders and self-care tips for follow-ups
Staff spend less time on routine queries

Future: More modules (vaccination drives, wellness camps), kiosks and campus portal access, post-visit feedback to improve responses, and small pilots each semester before scaling.
